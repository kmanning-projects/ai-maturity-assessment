Five dimensions, each scored Level 1–5. Use this rubric alongside the interactive tool to find your binding constraint and the concrete next move to unlock it.

Most organizations cluster at Level 2 overall — and are usually unevenly mature: a Level‑3 data org stuck at Level‑1 governance is completely typical. The average score hides the constraint. The per-dimension profile reveals it.

Scoring & interpretation

1
Rate each dimension 1–5 using the descriptors and evidence signals above. Use the interactive tool for an instant radar chart, or score manually against this rubric.
2
Plot the five scores as a radar chart. The shape matters more than the average — a flat polygon reveals a balanced org; a spiky one reveals where investment has concentrated and where it hasn't.
3
Find the binding constraint: the lowest dimension with the highest leverage on outcomes. Governance and Adoption most commonly constrain programs even when Data and Talent look strong — because ungoverned pilots that can't reach production are not value, they are cost.
4
Define the next level for the binding constraint: read the descriptor one level up. That is your concrete next investment — not a five-year transformation, a one-level lift on one dimension.
The recommendation rule: raise your weakest high-leverage dimension by one level. Explicitly stop over-investing in dimensions already ahead of your constraint. Most orgs over-invest in Data & Infrastructure while stuck at Level 2 on Governance and Adoption — the two dimensions that actually convert capability into shipped value.
